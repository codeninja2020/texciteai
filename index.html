South Africa has two chronically underutilised assets: distributed rooftop solar generation and secure, staffed community premises. SNAP XFRA turns both into an AI compute network. 03 — TECHNOLOGY

SNAP XFRA: the
distributed DC unit.

SNAP XFRA is a ruggedised, self-contained micro-datacenter for AI inference at the edge — silent, thermally managed, and solar-ready out of the box.

HARDWARE SPEC
GPU Compute 4–8× H100 / MI300X equiv.
Peak Draw 3.2 – 6.4 kW

Footprint 0.6m² (2U rack or pod)
Connectivity 10GbE / fibre / LTE backup
Power Input Grid + DC solar direct
☀️ Solar-first power architecture
Each unit prioritises on-site solar. Grid power fills gaps. When solar exceeds demand, surplus feeds back to grid or charges batteries — reducing net energy cost toward zero.
🌐 Mesh orchestration
All nodes connect to Texcite's central orchestration layer, routing inference jobs based on node availability, latency, and energy price signals.
📊 Real-time carbon telemetry
Each unit emits verifiable energy source data and compute hours on renewable power — forming the audit trail for certified carbon credits.

04 — HOST SEGMENTS

Who hosts the nodes.

Three primary host categories — each with distinct value propositions, procurement pathways, and ESG alignment. Hosts receive a revenue share and retain all carbon credits for their own reporting.

Schools & universities
Educational institutions are ideal first-movers: secure, monitored premises; many are already deploying solar under government mandates; sympathetic procurement processes; strong ESG narrative. 06 — CARBON CREDIT PROGRAM

Measurable green
compute credits.

The TexGreen Credit Standard
Every SNAP XFRA unit generates a verifiable stream of carbon displacement data: renewable energy consumed (kWh), carbon-equivalent displaced vs. grid average (kg CO₂e), and compute-hours delivered on clean power. Texcite aggregates this data and issues TexGreen Credits — audited, blockchain-anchored, and compatible with VERRA VCS and Gold Standard reporting frameworks.

💡 COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
Unlike renewable energy certificates (RECs) that simply claim clean energy was produced somewhere, TexGreen Credits prove that a specific AI workload was executed on green energy at an identifiable location. This makes TexGreen Credits the first compute-attributable carbon instrument — a far stronger ESG claim than a generic offset.
