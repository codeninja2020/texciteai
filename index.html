KEY INSIGHT
South Africa has two chronically underutilised assets: distributed rooftop solar generation and secure, staffed community premises. Texcite turns both into an AI compute network. By co-locating purpose-built inference nodes with solar at schools and hospitals, Texcite eliminates the largest cost in AI infrastructure — energy — while generating verifiable environmental value that companies will pay to claim.

The Texcite Node:
built for Africa.

Texcite designs and manufactures its own edge inference nodes — purpose-engineered for South African electrical standards, climate conditions, and connectivity realities. We don't depend on foreign product availability or international supply timelines. We build it here.

WHY TEXCITE BUILDS ITS OWN NODES
Centralised hyperscale datacenters take years to permit and billions to build. The distributed compute model changes that equation — but no existing product is engineered for African electrical standards, climate, or supply chain realities. Texcite builds from the ground up: sourcing proven commercial components (NVIDIA GPUs, Dell servers, SA-certified inverters and batteries), integrating them into a weatherised outdoor enclosure designed for our climate, and running our own orchestration software stack. This gives Texcite full control over hardware cost, supply chain, serviceability, and the roadmap — and makes us the only distributed edge compute company purpose-built for African conditions.

Solar-first power architecture
Each node prioritises on-site solar generation via DC-coupled integration. The 15 kWh LFP battery buffer handles load-shedding seamlessly — compute workloads migrate to healthy nodes during outages rather than dropping. Net energy cost approaches zero at high solar penetration sites.
TXOS orchestration layer
Texcite's proprietary TXOS (Texcite Orchestration System) routes inference jobs across the node fleet based on availability, latency requirements, and live energy price signals. Built on open standards — compatible with OpenAI API, Hugging Face, and vLLM inference engines.
Real-time carbon telemetry
Embedded energy metering logs kWh source (solar vs. grid) every 15 minutes in a tamper-evident, signed data stream. This forms the verifiable audit trail for TexGreen carbon credit issuance — no third-party meter required.
Local serviceability
Every component is sourced from SA-stocked distributors — Dell, NVIDIA, Victron, Pylontech. Field replacement takes under 2 hours. No international RMA cycles, no six-week wait for spares. Texcite trains and certifies its own field service technicians.

06 — CARBON CREDIT PROGRAM

Measurable green
compute credits.

The TexGreen Credit Standard
Every Texcite Node generates a verifiable stream of carbon displacement data: renewable energy consumed (kWh), carbon-equivalent displaced vs. grid average (kg CO₂e), and compute-hours delivered on clean power. Texcite aggregates this data and issues TexGreen Credits — audited, blockchain-anchored, and compatible with VERRA VCS and Gold Standard reporting frameworks.

COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
Unlike renewable energy certificates (RECs) that simply claim clean energy was produced somewhere, TexGreen Credits prove that a specific AI workload was executed on green energy at an identifiable location. This makes TexGreen Credits the first compute-attributable carbon instrument — a far stronger ESG claim than a generic offset.
